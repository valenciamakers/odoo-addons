Custom Colors

Make Odoo's colors your own, everywhere they appear

In standard Odoo, the eleven colors you can give a tag, a kanban card, or a calendar are fixed, and so are their names. Custom Colors lets you change any of them to one of your own, such as your brand's, and rename it, from Settings.

A changed color is used everywhere Odoo draws that color: tags and badges, kanban cards, calendars, the color picker, and the website. Records keep the colors they had, and simply show the new ones.

Fits right into Odoo

Every color picker shows your colors and your names

Pick each color with Odoo's own color picker, right in the list.

Rename a color in each of your languages, as any translatable name.

A tag's border, background, and text shades are worked out from your color, as Odoo works out its own.

On Enterprise, dark mode gets shades of its own, as Odoo's colors do.

Small and focused on purpose

Back to Odoo's colors in one click

The undo arrow on a changed color puts back Odoo's color and name, and Reset to Defaults does it for all eleven. Uninstalling the module does too. The slots stay Odoo's: this changes how each color looks and what it is called, not how many there are, and No color stays as it is.

This module requires nothing beyond Odoo's Settings, and works on Community and Enterprise alike.
