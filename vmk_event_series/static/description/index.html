Event Series

Group all dates of a monthly workshop, a weekly class, or a yearly meetup into one series

In standard Odoo, a workshop you run every month is a dozen unrelated events. Event Series solves this by grouping events into a series. Each individual date stays an ordinary event, with its own tickets, seats, and registrations, and the series simply groups them together.

Publishing events on your website? Use Event Series (Website) instead: it includes this module, and also gives each event series a page on your website, listing all of the upcoming dates.

Fits right into Odoo

See every date in a series, wherever you look for events

New Event in Series creates the next date in the series, named after the series and using its template.

Add an existing event to a series using the Series field on the Event form.

A series shows its next event, and a count of dates that have not ended.

Search and group events by series, and show a Series column in the events list.

Small and focused on purpose

A series just links your events together, changing nothing on the event itself

Templates are used to fill in a new event with default tickets, communications, and more, but can't be used to group events. A series is only a link: adding an event to a series, or moving it to another one, leaves its tickets and everything else as they were. A series can assign a template, which all new events in the series will start from.

This module only requires Events Organization, the backend part of the Events app, and by itself changes nothing on a website.
