Event Series

Keep the dates of a monthly workshop, a weekly class, or a yearly meetup together

In standard Odoo, a workshop you run every month is a dozen unrelated events. Event Series groups them into one series. Each date stays an ordinary event, with its own tickets, seats, and registrations, and the series keeps them together.

Publishing events on your website? Use Event Series (Website) instead: it includes this module, and also gives each series a page of its own on your website, listing its upcoming dates.

Fits right into Odoo

Find every date of a series, wherever you look for events

New Event in Series starts the next date already in the series, named after it and filled in from its template.

Add an existing event to a series with its Series field, beside the template on the event form.

A series shows its next event, and counts the dates that have not yet ended.

Search and group events by series, and show a Series column in the events list.

Small and focused on purpose

A series links your events, and changes nothing on them

Choosing a template fills in an event's tickets, communications, and more, so it can't be used to group events you already have. A series is only a link: adding an event to a series, or moving it to another one, leaves its tickets and everything else as they were. A series can still name a template, which its new dates start from.

This module only requires Events Organization, the backend part of the Events app, and by itself changes nothing on a website.
