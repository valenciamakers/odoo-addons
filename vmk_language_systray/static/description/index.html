Backend Language Menu

Switch your own backend language from the menu bar

In standard Odoo, changing your backend language requires opening your preferences, finding the language field, and saving. Backend Language Menu adds a globe to the backend menu bar: click it, pick a language, and the backend reloads in your new language.

Fits right into Odoo

Displays in the menu bar, beside the company switcher

Lists every enabled language, with your active language clearly marked.

Changes only your own backend language; other users and the website are unaffected.

Every backend user can use it, with no extra access rights required.

With Language Sequence installed, the menu follows your custom order.

Small and focused on purpose

Nothing to configure

The menu appears only when more than one language is enabled, and hides on phone-width screens, just like the company switcher does.

This module only requires Odoo's web module, and works on both Community and Enterprise.
