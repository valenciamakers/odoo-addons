Disable Visitor Tracking

Keep no record of who visits your website, or what they read

In standard Odoo, the Website app records every visitor and each page they view, with the country it looks up from their IP address, under Website › Reporting › Visitors. There is no setting to stop it, and the cookies bar doesn't either.

Disable Visitor Tracking switches it off on every website the moment it is installed, and adds a Track Visitors setting to turn it back on for any website that wants it.

Off means off

Nothing to configure, and nothing left recording in the background

No visitors and no page views are recorded, on any page, news article, or event.

Forms still work: a CRM lead or an event registration is made, just without a visitor.

Per website: tick Track Visitors on any website that wants Odoo's tracking back.

Uninstalling the module puts Odoo's own behavior back, with tracking on everywhere.

Small and focused on purpose

It stops new records, and leaves the rest to you

Visitors already recorded stay until you delete them, in Website › Reporting › Visitors. Analytics you add yourself, such as Google Analytics or Plausible, are separate settings and are not affected.

Two features need a visitor to work, so leave Track Visitors on for a website that uses them: push notifications (Enterprise's Social Marketing), and a visitor's favorite talks and reminders on an event's agenda (Advanced Events). The manual has the details.

This module only requires Odoo's Website app.
